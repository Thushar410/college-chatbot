## 2. Shree Devi Institute of Technology (SDIT) - Kenjar Campus
*   **Location:** Airport Road, Kenjar, Mangalore - 574 142, Karnataka State, India.
*   **Campus Contact:** +91 (824) 2254104 | **Email:** sditprincipal@gmail.com
*   **Affiliation & Approvals:** Affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU) and approved by the All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE).

### Academic Programs & Admission Eligibility
*   **Undergraduate Engineering (B.E - 4 Years):** Computer Science, CSE (AI & ML), AI & Data Science, Information Science, Electronics & Communication, Aeronautical, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering.
    *   **Eligibility:** Minimum 45% aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics at the 10+2 level. A relaxation to 40% is provided for SC/ST and OBC candidates of Karnataka.
    *   **Accepted Entrance Exams:** COMEDK UGET, KCET, JEE Main.
*   **Postgraduate Programs (2 Years):**
    *   **M.Tech & MBA:** Requires a minimum of 50% aggregate in a recognized Bachelor's Degree.
    *   **MCA:** Requires a BCA, B.Sc, B.Com, or B.A, preferably with Mathematics at the 10+2 or graduation level.
    *   **Accepted Entrance Exams (PG):** KMAT, Karnataka PGCET, and GATE.

### Training & Placements
*   **Training Initiatives:** The Training and Placement Department conducts Pre-Placement Training (aptitude, logical skills, and mock interviews), Personality Development Programs (PDP), and Bridge Courses.
*   **Industrial Exposure:** The college organizes vocational training, industrial visits, and project internships for 2nd and 3rd-year students to bridge the gap between theory and practice.
*   **Top Corporate Recruiters:** Infosys, Oracle, TCS, Amazon, Wipro, IBM, DXC Technology, Pin Click, Accolite Digital, and Mphasis.
