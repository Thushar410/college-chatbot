## Overview

### Master of Business Administration (MBA)

MBA program opens new career opportunities and provide for personal development of the students. An MBA is considered to be a quick passport to success in the increasingly competitive corporate world.

The MBA program at Shree Devi Institute of Technology stresses on managerial aspects of accounting, finance, marketing and management. Case studies and industrial visits incorporated into the syllabus to prepare students for managerial positions in small businesses as well as multinational companies. Transforming graduates into visionary individuals is the goal of the program.

### About the Department

The Department of Master of Business Administration at Shree Devi Institute of Technology is a hub of academic excellence and professional development. It is dedicated to producing highly skilled and ethical business leaders equipped to face global challenges. The department offers a curriculum that integrates theoretical knowledge with practical applications, ensuring students are industry-ready.

The department is known for its vibrant learning environment, which includes guest lectures by industry experts, hands-on workshops, and industrial visits to prominent companies. Students also benefit from various skill development initiatives, research opportunities, and participation in national level management fests. With a focus on holistic growth, the department emphasizes cultural, sports, and community engagement activities. The committed faculty members mentor students to excel academically and professionally, fostering a spirit of innovation and entrepreneurship.

## Eligibility

### Eligibility

A candidate who has passed Bachelor’s Degree examination or equivalent examination from recognized University of minimum of 3 years duration examination and obtained an aggregate minimum of 50% marks taken together in all the subjects including languages in all the years of the Degree Examination is eligible for admission to MBA programme.

#### Note:

45% of marks in Q. E. in case of SC, ST and Category – I of Karnataka candidates. Reservation and concession in aggregate percentage is applicable only for Karnataka candidates.

# Department Activities Overview

The Department of Master of Business Administration at Shree Devi Institute of Technology is committed to nurturing future business leaders through a blend of academic excellence, practical exposure, and holistic development. Below is an overview of the diverse activities conducted by the department:

### Meraki Association

Meraki serves as the umbrella association for various clubs and activities within the MBA Department, fostering holistic development and a sense of community among students. Under Meraki, several specialized clubs and initiatives are organized to cater to diverse interests and skillsets:

### HRiday – HR Club

The HR Club conducts activities focused on enhancing interpersonal and organizational skills. Through case studies, role-plays, and guest sessions, the club aims to prepare students for dynamic HR roles, fostering leadership and strategic thinking.

Echelon not only enhances the participants’ knowledge and strategic thinking but also prepares them for real-world business challenges.
