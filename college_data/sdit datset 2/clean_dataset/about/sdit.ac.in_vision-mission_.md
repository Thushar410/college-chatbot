# Vision & Mission

## Vision

To be an institution of academic excellence imparting technical and management education integrated with human values to face the social and environmental challenges.

## Mission

- Disseminate quality education with student centric-learning methodologies.
- Actively engage and encourage students, faculty and staff.
- Mentoring students and provide career development support.
- Impart education that transforms students and empower them to emerge as professionals by inculcating entrepreneurial attitudes with social and ethical values.
