# Affiliation and Recognition

## Affiliation

Shree Devi Institute of Technology is affiliated to Visveswaraya Technological University, Belgaum, Karnataka.

## Recognition

Shree Devi Institute of Technology is recognized by All India Council for Technical Education (AICTE), New Delhi.
