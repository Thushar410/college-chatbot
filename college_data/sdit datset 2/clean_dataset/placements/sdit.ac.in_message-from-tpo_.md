# Message from TPO

Dear Students, Faculty, Industry Partners, and Visitors,

Welcome to the Training and Placement Department of Shree Devi Institute of Technology (SDIT), where we are dedicated to empowering our students with the skills, knowledge, and opportunities they need to succeed in the professional world.

As the Training and Placement Officer, it is my privilege to lead a dynamic team committed to facilitating career readiness and enhancing employability among our students. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academia and industry by providing comprehensive training, career guidance, and placement assistance.

At SDIT, we recognize that academic excellence alone is not enough to thrive in today’s competitive job market. That’s why we offer a range of training programs, workshops, and seminars designed to enhance students’ technical competencies, soft skills, and professional demeanor. From technical certifications to mock interviews and resume building workshops, we provide students with the tools and resources they need to stand out to potential employers.

Our strong industry partnerships play a crucial role in our placement efforts. We collaborate closely with leading companies and organizations to facilitate internship opportunities, campus recruitment drives, and industry interactions. These partnerships not only provide students with valuable hands-on experience but also offer insights into industry trends and requirements.

Our placement record speaks volumes about the caliber of our students and the effectiveness of our training and placement initiatives. Year after year, our graduates secure placements in top-tier companies across various sectors, ranging from IT and engineering to finance and consulting.

Whether you are a student exploring internship opportunities, a recent graduate seeking employment, or an employer looking to recruit talented individuals, the Training and Placement section is here to assist you. We are committed to providing personalized guidance and support to help you achieve your career goals.

I encourage you to explore the Training and Placement section of our website to learn more about our programs, services, and upcoming placement activities. Together, let us embark on a journey of professional growth and success.

Thank you for choosing Shree Devi Institute of Technology as your partner in career development. We look forward to helping you realize your full potential and achieve your dreams.

**Warm regards,**

**Roshal Lynshal Nazareth**

Training and Placement Officer, Shree Devi Institute of Technology.
