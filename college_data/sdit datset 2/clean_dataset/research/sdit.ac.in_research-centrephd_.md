## Overview

### Overview

Research is an inevitable arm of modern educational system, the only channel which enables knowledge generation. Having our motive to provide uncompromising quality education, the SDIT had established the Research Centre to facilitate research and knowledge generation and to identify itself in International arena.

The Physics Research Centre facilitates strong Doctoral Program and research projects with very active national and International research collaboration programs.
