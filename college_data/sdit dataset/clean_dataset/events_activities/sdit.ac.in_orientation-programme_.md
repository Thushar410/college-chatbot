#### Orientation Programme

![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/orientation-program.jpg)

The orientation program is a crucial component of the college induction process, designed to familiarize new students with the college environment, resources, and academic expectations. It provides an opportunity for incoming students to meet faculty members, administrators, and fellow classmates, as well as to learn about the college’s facilities, programs, and support services. Orientation sessions typically include informative presentations, campus tours, and interactive activities aimed at easing the transition to college life and promoting a sense of belonging and community among new students.
