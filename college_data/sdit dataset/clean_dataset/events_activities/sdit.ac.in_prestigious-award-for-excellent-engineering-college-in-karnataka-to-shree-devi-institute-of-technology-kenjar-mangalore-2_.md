#### Prestigious Award for “Excellent Engineering College in Karnataka” to Shree Devi Institute of Technology, Kenjar Mangalore

![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/shree-devi-event.jpg)

Shree Devi Institute of Technology, Kenjar, Mangalore was conferred with the prestigious award for “Excellent Engineering College in Karnataka” at the 2nd National Karnataka Education Summit & Awards 2016 organized by Visvesvaraya Technological University, Belagavi in association with CMAI Association of India. The award function was held at Visvesvaraya Technological University Center for PG Studies, Muddenahalli, Bangalore on 14th March, 2016.

Sri R.V. Deshpande, Minister for Large and Medium Scale Industries and Dept. of Tourism, Govt. of Karnataka and Sri T.B. Jayachandra, Minister for Law, Parliamentary Affairs and Higher Education, Govt. of Karnataka were the Chief Guests. Dr. K. Sudhakar, M.L.A., Chikkaballapura was the Guest of Honour, Dr. H. Maheshappa, Vice Chancellor, Visvesvaraya Technological University presided over the function. Sri N.K. Goyal, President, CMAI Association of India and Mr. T Bhaskara Executive Engineer (E),Technical Advisor and Special Officer Visvesvaraya Technological University regional office Mysorewere present on the occasion.

Sri A. Sadananda Shetty Chairman, Shree Devi Education Trust, Mr. Nidhish Shetty Vice Chairman and Dr.Dilip Kumar Principal received the award from Sri R.V. Deshapnde. The award was presented to Shree Devi Institute of Technology recognizing its excellence in various parameters such as infrastructure, teaching, industry-academia interface and placement efforts.

North Dakota State University Tie-Up with Shree Devi Institute of Technology Kenjar Mangalore: Shree Devi Institute of Technology Kenjar Mangalore in accordance with a mutual objective to promote cooperation between North Dakota State University, United Stated of America Jointly entered into a bilateral linkage, focusing on the development of their institutions’ programs. The courses under Shree Devi Education Trust are jointly entered with the North Dakota State University (NDSU) U.S.A by signing a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU). Shree Devi Institute of Technology Kenjar Mangalore is the second institute in the country to have the tie up with the prestigious North Dakota State of University(NDSU), USA.
