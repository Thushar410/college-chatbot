# Eligibility

# Bachelor of Engineering

### Eligibility

Pre-University Board or any other examinations recognized by the University or equivalent thereto, with a minimum of 45%\* aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics.

**Note:**

\*40% for students belonging to SC/ST and OBC categories.

Documents required :

- Original **Secondary School Leaving Certificate (SSLC)**
- Original **Pre-University Certificate (PUC)**
- Photocopy of **Adhar card**
- Original Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct Certificate
- Passport size photo of applicant
- Migration certificate (for non-Karnataka students)

# MBA

### Eligibility

A candidate who has passed Bachelor’s Degree examination or equivalent examination from recognized University of minimum of 3 years duration examination and obtained an aggregate minimum of 50% marks taken together in all the subjects including languages in all the years of the Degree Examination is eligible for admission to MBA programme.

**Note:**

45% of marks in Q. E. in case of SC, ST and Category – I of Karnataka candidates. Reservation and concession in aggregate percentage is applicable only for Karnataka candidates.

# MCA

### Eligibility

A candidate who has passed any recognized BCA / Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent Degree Or Passed B.Sc. / B.Com / B.A, preferably with Mathematics at 10 + 2 level or at Graduation level \[For the students having no Mathematics background bridge courses will have to be studied and passed as notified by the University) and obtained an aggregate minimum of 50% marks taken together in all the subjects in all the years of the Degree Examination is eligible for admission to MCA courses.\
\
**Note:**\
\
\*45% of marks in Q. E. in case of SC, ST and Category-I of Karnataka candidates.\
\
# MTech\

### Eligibility\

A candidate who has passed qualifying examination or equivalent examination as prescribed by the Competent Authority and obtained an aggregate minimum of 50% marks taken together in all the subjects of all years / semesters of the Degree Examination is eligible for admission to M.E / M.Tech / M.Arch courses.\

**Note:**\
