## Overview

### About Master of Computer Applications (MCA)

The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program at Shree Devi Institute of Technology is designed to equip students with advanced knowledge and skills in computer science, programming, and software development. A two year’s full-time course includes interactive classes, national seminars, outbound activities and emphasizes practical experience through projects and internships. Upon completion, graduates are prepared for careers in software development, IT consulting, and systems analysis.

### Vision:

Empowering students to become skilled professionals and leaders in the dynamic field of technology fostering team work and ethical practices.

### Mission:

- Cultivating ethical and social responsible individuals for positive contribution to society
- Mentoring individuals by providing career support and development
- Providing opportunities for technical efficacy and team work

### Program Outcomes (POs)

- **PO1: Foundation Knowledge:** Solve problems effectively by using knowledge of mathematics, programming, and coding to design solutions.
- **PO2: Problem Analysis:** Analyze and understand problems by reviewing customer requirements and applying critical thinking to develop effective solutions.
- **PO3: Development of Solutions:** The program will design and develop innovative solutions that address key ESG/SDG challenges, fostering sustainable and impactful outcomes.
- **PO4: Modern Tool Usage:** The program will apply modern computational tools to develop algorithms, while addressing limitations like human biases and data constraints.
- **PO5: Individual and Teamwork:** The program will enable effective individual and team collaboration in diverse, multidisciplinary groups, utilizing agile methodologies for successful project execution.
- **PO6: Project Management and Finance:** The program will apply project management principles like scheduling and work breakdown structure, while integrating financial knowledge to ensure profitable and efficient project execution.
- **PO7: Ethics:** The program will uphold professional ethics in managing software projects with financial aspects, while leveraging new technologies to enhance cybersecurity and protect customers from malware.
- **PO8: Life-long learning:** The program will foster change management skills and the ability to continuously learn, adapt to new technologies, and stay current with evolving work practices.

### Program Educational Objectives(PEO’s):

- **PEO 1:** Providing in-depth knowledge of fundamental concepts and basics of programming skills.
- **PEO 2:** Will be able to use the new technologies to meet the evolving demands in the IT industry
- **PEO 3:** To embed professional and ethical values, leadership quality and team work

## Eligibility

### Eligibility

A candidate who has passed any recognized BCA / Bachelor Degree in Computer Science Engineering or equivalent Degree Or Passed B.Sc. / B.Com / B.A, preferably with Mathematics at 10 + 2 level or at Graduation level \[For the students having no Mathematics background bridge courses will have to be studied and passed as notified by the University) and obtained an aggregate minimum of 50% marks taken together in all the subjects in all the years of the Degree Examination is eligible for admission to MCA courses.\
\
#### Note:\
\
\*45% of marks in Q. E. in case of SC, ST and Category-I of Karnataka candidates.\
\

## Activities\
\
### Activities\
\
The Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program at Shree Devi Institute of Technology is a postgraduate course designed to equip students withquality pedagogy, hands on experience workshops and practical exposure to project development. It covers areas like software development, database management, networking, and programming languages. The curriculum also includes practical training, project work, and internships. Graduates are prepared for careers in IT, software development, and system administration.\
\
**SPARKLE:** Association is a student-driven initiative within the Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program, aimed at fostering innovation and collaboration. It organizes workshops, technical eventsand seminars to enhance learning, team building and industry exposure. The association also promotes networking opportunities and skill development for MCA students.\
\
**Independence Day Charity Event:** The Independence Day Charity Event organized by the MCA department aims to celebrate the spirit of freedom while contributing to a noble cause. Students and faculty come together to raise funds through donations, which are then directed towards supporting Chitanya Ashram, a place dedicated to the welfare of underprivileged individuals. This event not only fosters a sense of social responsibility but also highlights the importance of giving back to the community in honor of India’s independence.\
\
![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/mca-activity1.jpg)\
\
**Social Out Reach Program:** The Social Outreach Program organized by the MCA department includes a visit to a government school, where students actively engage with children to support their learning and development. During the visit, students conduct educational activities such as providing study materials, and share knowledge about technology. This initiative aims to foster social responsibility, inspire young minds, and bridge the educational gap in underprivileged communities.\
\
![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/mca-activity2.jpg)\
\
**Industrial Visits:** from the college offers students practical exposure to real-world applications of their academic learning. It provides an opportunity to interact with industry professionals, understand operational processes, and explore the latest technologies in the field. Such visits help bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and industry practices. Students gain insights that aid in career preparation and enhance their overall educational experience.\
\
![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/mca-activity3.jpg)\
\
**Medha:** is a national level technical fest organized by the MCA department, which is aimed at promoting innovation, creativity, and technical skills amongst students. The event features various competitions across different domains of technology and engineering. It serves as a platform for students to enhance their skills like team management, communication, leadership and gain hands-on experience in event management. Medha fosters a spirit of collaboration and learning, bringing together participants from colleges across the country.\
\

**Outbound Training:** for college students is an experiential learning program designed to develop essential skills like leadership, teamwork, and problem-solving. The training typically involves outdoor activities, adventure sports, and group challenges that encourage personal growth and effective communication. It helps students build resilience, confidence, and decision-making abilities. Such programs provide a dynamic environment for students to learn outside the traditional classroom setting.\
\
![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/mca-activity7.jpg)\
\
**Cyber Security Workshop:** A Cyber Security Workshop conducted for MCA students to provide hands-on experience and in-depth knowledge of various cybersecurity practices, tools, and techniques. The workshop covers topics such as network security, ethical hacking, malware analysis, and data protection strategies, equipping students with the skills to protect against cyber threats. It also emphasizes the importance of staying updated with the latest security trends to ensure a secure digital environment in both personal and professional spheres.\
\
![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/mca-activity8.jpg)\
\
**IOT Workshop:** An IoT Workshop is conducted for MCA students to understand the fundamental concepts and applications of the Internet of Things, focusing on connecting physical devices to the internet for smart data collection and automation. The workshop includes hands-on sessions on building IoT systems, using sensors, and implementing cloud-based solutions for real-time data analysis. Students gain practical insights into IoT protocols, security challenges, and emerging trends, preparing them for careers in this rapidly growing field.\
\
![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/mca-activity9.jpg)\
\
**NIRVAN:** Is an Alumni Association in a Master of Computer Applications (MCA) program which serves as a platform to maintain strong connections between the graduates and the current students of the program. It helps foster networking, mentorship, and knowledge sharing, enabling alumni to guide and inspire the next generation of professionals. The association also organizes events, reunions, and career development opportunities to strengthen the community and provide support for career growth and industry connections.\
\
**Salesforce Technical Seminar:** A Salesforce Marketing Cloud technical seminar was carried out by Ms. Rakshitha P(Capgemini) proud alumni for MCA students. The session included a comprehensive understanding of how to use Salesforce’s powerful marketing automation platform to enhance customer engagement. The workshop covers key features such as email marketing, social media integration, customer journey mapping, and data-driven decision-making. Students gain practical experience in leveraging Marketing Cloud to create personalized, effective campaigns, preparing them for roles in digital marketing and customer relationship management.\
\
![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/mca-activity10.jpg)\
\
**Traditional Day:** The Traditional Day celebration in the MCA department offers students an opportunity to showcase and embrace their cultural heritage by dressing in traditional attire. The event features a variety of cultural performances, games, and activities that bring students and faculty together, fostering a sense of unity and community. It serves as a fun and meaningful way to celebrate diversity while strengthening the bond within the department.\
\
![](https://sdit.ac.in/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/mca-activity11.jpg)\
\
**Extension Activities:** Extension activities in the MCA department aim to extend the learning experience beyond the classroom by engaging students in real-world applications of their knowledge. These activities include organizing workshops, technical talks, coding competitions, internships, and community service projects, helping students develop practical skills and contribute to societal development. Additionally, collaborations with industry experts and academic institutions provide students with valuable exposure to emerging technologies and trends in the field of computer science.\
