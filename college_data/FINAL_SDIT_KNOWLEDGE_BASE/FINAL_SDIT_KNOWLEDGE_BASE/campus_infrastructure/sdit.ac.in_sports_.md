# Sports

The college provides adequate facilities for sporting activities that are necessary to encourage the all-round development of the students. The college sports teams have represented the college in various state and national level competitions and have brought home laurels. The college also hosts a state- level, intercollegiate tournament which is very popular with the student fraternity.
