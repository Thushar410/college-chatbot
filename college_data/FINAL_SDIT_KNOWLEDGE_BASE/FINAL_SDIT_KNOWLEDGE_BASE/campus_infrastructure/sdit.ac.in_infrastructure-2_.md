# Infrastructure

Spacious classrooms are designed to facilitate discussions, audio visual presentations and practical demonstrations. Wi-Fi classrooms enable students to source information from the internet for more effective learning. These audio-visual aids are a boon to teachers and students alike. The Seminar Halls are equipped with modern audio-visual equipment, LCD and slide projectors .Each student is given customized attention. Brainstorming is encouraged and practiced in small alcoves projecting out of these rooms.

Edusat facility enables the students to learn from online classes conducted by their professors and other eminent personalities. Our highly qualified and experienced faculty members are also available after class hours for the students who seek extra guidance. Various speakers from the industry as well as academics are invited to interact with students and share their experiences.
