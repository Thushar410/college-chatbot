## Overview

### Overview

Shree Devi Institute of technology instituted The Department of **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science** in the curriculum owing to the increasing demand for computer professionals and software developers in very field of business. The department offers a 4 years B.E degree in of **Artificial Intelligence & Data Science.** With world class training and research, the department encourages students to experiment and take part in sponsored research projects through industry-institute collaboration. The department provides state-of-the-art facilities to the students to enable them to be toppers in their chosen vocations.

## Eligibility

### Eligibility

Pre-University Board or any other examinations recognized by the University or equivalent thereto, with a minimum of 45%\* aggregate marks in Physics, Chemistry & Mathematics.

#### Note:

\*40% for students belonging to SC/ST and OBC categories.

#### Documents required

- Original **Secondary School Leaving Certificate (SSLC)**
- Original **Pre-University Certificate (PUC)**
- Photocopy of **Adhar card**

- Original Transfer Certificate (TC) and Conduct Certificate
- Passport size photo of applicant
- Migration certificate (for non-Karnataka students)
