SDIT with all of its Lecturers, Staff and Students, has aided in my Academics by providing me with a unique perspective an curriculum and exhilarating engineering program. I must say, Events, Cultural Celebrations and College Infrastructure are commendable. Kudos to the SDIT Management for all the support, exposure and opportunities that you offered us throughout our college life. I think the Institute, Placements cell and the ECE Dept. for all the efforts put in by them, along with the perseverance and right moves have paid off finally during campus placements. I got placed in Infoview Technologies (Japanese MNC) with 6.5 LPA starting package and then over years my career took a good leap by Gods grace. Currently I am working with Etisalat (UAE Government Telecommunications), as a Software Engineer.
